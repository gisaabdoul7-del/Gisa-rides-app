# Gisa Rides

Interactive demo · Kigali, Rwanda

## Try a full ride

1. On **Passenger**, pick a pickup and destination, choose Moto or Car, and request the ride.
2. Switch to **Driver** and accept the request. If you wait, another driver accepts for you.
3. Switch back to watch the driver arrive, give the trip code, ride, then pay and rate.
4. Open **Admin** to see the ride, the commission, and to change fares. Quotes update at once.

This demo runs in your browser with a simulated map, drivers and payments. Nothing is sent anywhere. The real app uses Flutter, Supabase and Google Maps for live GPS, accounts and ride matching.
